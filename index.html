            How to play

            Match the top card by colour or symbol. Tap a card, or drag it up onto the table. Nothing to play? Draw from the pile.

            Skip: the next player misses a turn. Reverse: play goes the other way. Draw 2: the next player draws two. Colour: choose the next colour. Draw 4: choose a colour and the next player draws four, but it can be challenged.

            Press LAST CARD! when you are about to have one card left. Forget, and everyone else gets 7 seconds to press CATCH. If they catch you, you draw two. Press LAST CARD! before they do to save yourself. A hand you got from a swap never needs the call.

            Round score is the value of the cards left in opponents' hands: numbers count face value, action cards 20, wilds 50.
